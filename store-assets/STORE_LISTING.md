### Description

Leave Me Alone Games is a quiet collection of 31 familiar games that can be played without ads, accounts, subscriptions, tracking, or interruptions.

Play solitaire and puzzles on your own, challenge adjustable computer opponents, or share one device for local play. Pass-and-play card games hide each hand until the next player deliberately reveals it.

The collection includes Klondike, FreeCell, Spider, Pyramid, Tri-Peaks, Golf, Yukon, Rummy, Gin Rummy, Hearts, Spades, 83-Maine's Card Game, Cribbage, Chess, Checkers, Mahjong, Dominoes, Reversi, Backgammon, Connect 4, Tic-Tac-Toe, Yacht, Farkle, Shut the Box, Sudoku, Kakuro, Peg Solitaire, Mastermind, Nonograms, 2048, and Lights Out.

Highlights:

- 31 games in one app
- Adjustable computer difficulty in opponent games
- Local two-, three-, and four-player modes where supported
- Private handoff screens for pass-and-play card games
- Multiple comfortable color themes
- Ten interface languages
- Offline native play
- No ads, accounts, analytics, tracking, subscriptions, or in-app purchases

Your settings and game progress stay on your device. Leave Me Alone Games is built for people who want to open a game, play quietly, and be left alone.

### What’s New in 3.2.0

Added stronger computer play and difficulty choices across the newest card games, including Rummy, Gin Rummy, Hearts, Spades, Cribbage, and 83-Maine's Card Game. Hearts now includes four-player passing, Spades includes nil and bag scoring, Cribbage has improved crib and pegging decisions, and pass-and-play hands remain hidden until each player reveals them. This release also adds a perfect-play Tic-Tac-Toe level and completes the iOS and Android publishing setup.
