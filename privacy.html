Privacy Policy

Effective August 13, 2026

Leave Me Alone Games does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal data. The app has no accounts, advertisements, analytics, tracking technology, remote database, social features, or third-party advertising SDKs.

Information stored on your device

The app uses local browser storage for preferences such as background, language, difficulty, favorites, and selected game settings. Some games also keep the current game or session so play can continue on the same device. This information is not sent to the developer or to another company.

Network access

The native iOS and Android apps package the games on the device and do not require a network connection to play. The web version loads its own app files from the site where it is hosted and does not contact advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

Children

The collection contains general-audience card, board, dice, solitaire, and puzzle games. Because the app does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children or adults.

Your choices

You can remove locally saved preferences and game state with Reset App Data on the main menu, by clearing the app’s storage, or by uninstalling the app.

Support and privacy questions

Use the project’s support page for help or privacy questions. If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated before the revised app is published.
